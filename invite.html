Your friend is on Pour

Pour is an iPhone app for ranking the wines you try and seeing how your friends rank them. It's in a small test, so you get it through Apple's TestFlight app.

  1. Get Pour on TestFlight: the test opens soon. Check back on this page.
  2. Open Pour, confirm you're 21 or older, and sign in with Apple.
  3. When Pour asks "Who invited you?", type your friend's username. You'll be friends right away, and it helps them unlock new share themes.

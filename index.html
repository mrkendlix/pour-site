Rank wines you try. See how your friends rank them.

Pour is an iPhone app for ranking the wines you try. Pick which one you liked more, and Pour gives each wine a score out of 10, with your friends' rankings right next to yours.

How it works

  1. Rank it. Loved it, it was fine, or not for me. Then a few quick "which did you like more?" taps.
  2. Get your list. Every wine gets a score, ranked against the others in its category.
  3. Compare with friends. See what they loved, and how closely your taste matches theirs.

Privacy policy

Effective October 2026

Pour is an iPhone app for ranking the wines you try and comparing with friends. We collect as little as we can, never sell it, and never use it for ads.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Pour doesn't ask Apple for your name or email; we only get an anonymous account ID.
  • Your username, which you choose.
  • Your date of birth, only to check you're 21 or older.
  • What you do in Pour: the wines you rank and their scores, wines you save to Want to try, wines you add, your friends and friend requests, the badges and streak you earn, and people you block or report.
  • Basic usage events, such as "ranked a wine" or "opened the app", with the time. We use these to learn whether Pour is working. They're stored with your account, not shared with anyone.
  • A push notification token, only if you turn on the Monday heads-up.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or anything from other apps. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in to Pour can see your username, so friends can find you.
  • Your rankings, Want to try list, badges and streak are visible only to you and the friends you accept.
  • Your date of birth isn't shown to anyone, including your friends.
  • Wines you add by hand join Pour's shared wine list (the wine's name, producer, grape and country). Pour doesn't show who added a wine.
  • Reports go only to the Pour team.

Where it's kept

Pour's data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which keeps it on our behalf. Push notifications go through Apple and Expo's notification service, which only receive your push token and the message.

Deleting your data

In Pour, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything listed above for good, and ends Pour's access to your Apple sign-in. Reports you sent about other people are kept without your name, so we can still act on them. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Age

Pour is for people 21 and older. If you're under 21, Pour won't create an account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change matters.

Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026

Pour is an iPhone app for ranking the wines you try and comparing with friends. We collect as little as we can, never sell or share anything that identifies you, and never use your data for ads. We do share and sell wine insights made from totals across many people; see Wine insights.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Pour doesn't ask Apple for your name or email; we only get an anonymous account ID.
  • Your username, which you choose, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Your date of birth, only to check you're 21 or older.
  • What you do in Pour: the wines you rank and their scores, tastings you log (the year on the label, the date, label photos and notes), likes and comments on friends' rankings, wines you save to Want to try, wines you add, your friends and friend requests, the badges and streak you earn, and people you block or report.
  • Basic usage events, such as "ranked a wine" or "opened the app", with the time. We use these to learn whether Pour is working. They're stored with your account and never shared in a way that identifies you.
  • A push notification token, only if you turn on the Monday heads-up.
  • Photos you choose. Pour uses your camera or photo library only when you add a label photo to a tasting, and uploads only the photo you pick.
  • Not your contacts. If you use "Invite from your contacts", Pour reads the phone number of the one person you pick, only to open Messages with your invite. It isn't sent to us or saved.

We don't collect your location, your contacts, photos you haven't picked, or anything from other apps. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in to Pour can see your username and profile photo (if you add one), so friends can find you, and your place on the All members leaderboard (how many different grapes and countries you've ranked, nothing else).
  • Your rankings, Want to try list, badges and streak are visible only to you and the friends you accept.
  • Your date of birth isn't shown to anyone, including your friends.
  • Wines you add by hand join Pour's shared wine list (the wine's name, producer, grape and country). Pour doesn't show who added a wine.
  • Friends can see the year and date of each tasting you log. Your label photos and notes are private unless you switch on "Friends can see photos and notes" for that tasting.
  • Likes and comments on a friend's ranking are visible to that friend and their friends (not to people either of you has blocked).
  • Reports go only to the Pour team.

Wine insights

Pour learns what wine drinkers like, and we share that with the world: in social media posts, and with wineries, wine shops and others in the wine business, who may pay for it. These insights are always totals and percentages across many people, never about one person. For example: "62% of Pour users rank a Cabernet Sauvignon as their favorite red," or "the most-saved rosés this summer."

  • What goes into them: the wines people rank and their scores, the grapes, countries, regions and types of those wines, the years and dates of tastings, Want to try saves, and general app activity.
  • What never does: your username, your account, your friends, your comments, your tasting notes and label photos, or anything else that could identify you.
  • Always groups: every number comes from at least 25 people. We don't share or sell records about individual people, even with names removed.
  • No re-identifying: we don't try to work out who is behind an insight, and anyone we share insights with must agree not to try either.

Where it's kept

Pour's data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which keeps it on our behalf. Push notifications go through Apple and Expo's notification service, which only receive your push token and the message.

Deleting your data

In Pour, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything listed above for good, and ends Pour's access to your Apple sign-in. Reports you sent about other people are kept without your name, so we can still act on them. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Age

Pour is for people 21 and older. If you're under 21, Pour won't create an account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
